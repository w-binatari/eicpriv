Programme 01

Entrepreneurship And Digital Skills Training

Build enterprise mindset and digital capabilities that help individuals start, run, and grow viable businesses.

Our entrepreneurship and vocational skills training is built on venture creation and competent human capital for jobs or entrepreneurship pathways. We equip willing individuals with the mindset, skills, and certification to harness opportunities.

ESDP for Tertiary Institutions

An integral part of the school curriculum and a requirement for graduation — entrepreneurship theory and soft skills for job readiness or self-reliance. In partnership with Rivers State University, we run the yearly Students V-STEP programme.

Certiport Authorized Testing Center

As a Certiport Authorized Testing Center (a Pearson VUE Business), we offer digital literacy learning, practice testing, and certification. Training begins with a baseline assessment of current competence.

  • IC3 Digital Literacy
  • Google IT Support
  • IBM Data Analytics
  • Cyber Security
  • Microsoft Office Specialist
  • Entrepreneurship and Small Business

Programme 05

HSEQ Services

Health, Safety, Environment, and Quality services that support compliance and operational excellence.

From corporate policy and risk management to facility-level outsourcing, our solutions improve efficiency, reduce risk, and add business value.

What We Do

Practical training, consulting, and enterprise support — and the delivery models behind each programme.

Entrepreneurship & Digital Skills Technical & Vocational Management Consulting Business Linkages HSEQ Clarity4D Incubation

01

Entrepreneurship And Digital Skills Training

Build enterprise mindset and digital capabilities that help individuals start, run, and grow viable businesses.

Our entrepreneurship and vocational skills training is built on our drive towards ensuring venture creation and the availability of competent human capital for jobs or entrepreneurship pathways. We equip identified and willing individuals with the requisite mindset, skills, and certification to enable them harness opportunities.

Entrepreneurship & Skills Development Program (ESDP)

An integral part of the school curriculum and a requirement for graduation. It combines entrepreneurship theory and soft skills so undergraduate youths leave ready for jobs or self-reliance through entrepreneurial practice.

E&I Centre Limited in partnership with Rivers State University runs a yearly Vocational Skills Training and Entrepreneurship Promotion Program (Students V-STEP) for her students.

Certiport Authorized Testing Center

As a Certiport Authorized Testing Center (a Pearson VUE Business), we offer emerging digital literacy skills career certification — learning, practice testing, and certification for careers in this digital era. Training begins with a baseline assessment of current digital literacy and competence.

  • IC3 Digital Literacy Foundation / Basic IT
  • Google IT Support Professional
  • IBM Data Analytics
  • Cyber Security
  • Autodesk
  • Microsoft Certified Educator
  • QuickBooks
  • Microsoft Office Specialist
  • Entrepreneurship and Small Business (ESB)
  • Communication Skills for Business (CSB)

And other digital skills pathways across Education, IT, Communication, Business Management, Account/Finance, Legal/Admin, Engineering, and Health careers.

02

Technical And Vocational Skills Training

Hands-on technical and vocational programmes that strengthen employability and workplace readiness.

Professional Construction Skills Training

As a Training, Business Linkages and Job Creation Organisation, we partner with strategic stakeholders in the Construction Industry and Rivers State University to run a competency-based, demand-driven skills development programme.

  • Carpentry / Form Work
  • Plumbing
  • Tiling / Masonry
  • Welding & Fabrication
  • Electrical Wiring / Installation
  • Scaffolding

With entrepreneurship and soft skills training across board.

03

Sustainable Development and Management Consulting

Advisory support that helps organisations improve performance, sustainability, and management practice.

E&I Centre Ltd. understands the Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs) and can facilitate communities, organisations, and individuals to achieve set objectives and targets.

We also specialise in Management Consultancy to help organisations improve their performance and growth.

04

Business Linkages Facilitation

Connect enterprises to markets, partners, and opportunities that unlock growth and job creation.

LED (Local Enterprise Development) & Business Linkages

Working with clients and strategic partners, we build the capacity of enterprises within urban and rural areas, equipping them with entrepreneurship competence and access to finance. This serves as a socioeconomic development catalyst to reduce poverty by increasing income through employment generation and economic empowerment.

Business Mentoring Workshops

We offer Business Linkage Facilitation and mentoring workshops to link individuals or SMEs to job opportunities. The programme focuses on:

  • Building SME capacity to competitively meet specific market opportunities.
  • Increasing linkages between skilled individuals seeking employment and SMEs seeking new markets.
  • Improving access to finance.

05

HSEQ Services

Health, Safety, Environment, and Quality services that support compliance and operational excellence.

E&I Centre Ltd., in collaboration with its partner company Herock Integrated Safety Services Ltd, delivers innovative, cost-effective health, safety, environment and quality consulting services to various clients, particularly operators in the Nigerian Oil & Gas downstream sector.

From corporate-level HSEQ policy setting and risk management, to facility-level HSEQ outsourcing initiatives and support, our client-focused HSE solutions improve operational efficiency, reduce risk, and add true business value.

06

Clarity4D Profiling and Workshop

Personality profiling and facilitated workshops that improve teamwork, leadership, and communication.

Clarity4D — clear colourful communication

Our Clarity4D Profiling and Workshop/Training is for Human Resource Managers, Heads of Departments, team members, individuals, independent trainers, or resource persons in an organisation. It strengthens Leadership Effectiveness, Team Effectiveness, Emotional Intelligence, Personal Effectiveness, Problem Solving, Effective Communication, Effective Customer Service Delivery, and Stress & Conflict Management.

A centre for enterprise development in Port Harcourt

Entrepreneurship & Innovation Centre Ltd is a human capital development and management consultancy providing business and training services for economic empowerment and employment generation through local enterprise development and business linkages.

Affiliated with Rivers State University, we work with individuals, startups, and organisations to strengthen skills, improve readiness, and create sustainable jobs.

Our mandate

Create sustainable jobs in Nigeria through local enterprise development and business linkages — as a centre for entrepreneurial education, innovation support, and capacity development.

  • OGTAN registered training provider
  • Official consultant for TIIC
  • Strategic partner, Startup Science Tech Ideas Portal
  • Academic enrichment with Rivers State University

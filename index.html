Building the Next Generation of Innovators

Entrepreneurship & Innovation Centre Ltd (E&I Centre Ltd) is a professional Human Capital Development (HCD) and management consultancy company that provides business and training services for economic empowerment and employment generation through local enterprise development and business linkages.

We leverage innovation-driven entrepreneurship and strategic partnership to achieve sustainable economic development and wealth creation.

Vision

To become a centre of excellence for developing highly competent human capital that will be economically self-reliant and employable through job and business linkages.

Mission

Creating sustainable jobs in Nigeria through Local Enterprise Development and Business Linkages.

Our Core Values

Our core values are deeply rooted in our desire to develop human capital and create sustainable jobs.

From Ideas to Ventures

Our venture creation work equips individuals and enterprises with the mindset, skills, and pathways to start and grow sustainable businesses, creating jobs through local enterprise development and business linkages.

Programmes include entrepreneurship and skills development training, pre-retirement entrepreneurship pathways, SME capacity building, and access to finance facilitation for urban and rural enterprises.

  • Entrepreneurship & Skills Development Programme (ESDP)
  • Pre-Retirement Entrepreneurship Training (PRET)
  • Local Enterprise Development and business linkage facilitation
  • SME readiness for market opportunities and investment

Entrepreneurship & Innovation Centre Ltd (EIC) is a human capital development and management consultancy. We deliver business and training services for economic empowerment and employment generation through local enterprise development and business linkages.

Our mandate

Create sustainable jobs in Nigeria through local enterprise development, serving as a centre for entrepreneurial education, innovation support, and capacity development.

  • OGTAN registered training provider
  • Official consultant for TIIC
  • Strategic partner, Startup Science Tech Ideas Portal

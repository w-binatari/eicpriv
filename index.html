Our Leadership

Leadership

Engr. Chika Oluremi Chinwah

Managing Partner/CEO

Engr. Chinwah is a seasoned Techpreneur, Sustainable Development Expert, and certified Digital Skills for Entrepreneurs trainer with over 30 years of cross-sector experience.

Connect on LinkedIn

He has 15 years of technical experience in Oil & Gas operations with TotalEnergies, and 15 years leading business development, incubation, and venture creation across Nigeria.

As a COREN-registered Engineer and distinguished member of the Nigerian Society of Engineers, he currently serves as Chairman, Oil and Gas Trainers Association of Nigeria - OGTAN, Port Harcourt Zone. He is also a certified Digital Skills trainer and Human Capital Development specialist.

Engr. Chinwah holds a B.Eng from Federal University of Technology Owerri - FUTO and an MBA in Finance from University of Port Harcourt - UniPort Business School. He is also an alumnus of the MIT Entrepreneurship Program and the Oxford Saïd Venture Creation Program.

From Ideas to Ventures

Our venture creation work equips individuals and enterprises with the mindset, skills, and pathways to start and grow sustainable businesses, creating jobs through local enterprise development and business linkages.

Programmes include entrepreneurship and skills development training, pre-retirement entrepreneurship pathways, SME capacity building, and access to finance facilitation for urban and rural enterprises.

  • Entrepreneurship & Skills Development Programme (ESDP)
  • Pre-Retirement Entrepreneurship Training (PRET)
  • Local Enterprise Development and business linkage facilitation
  • SME readiness for market opportunities and investment

Innovation Support for Builders

Our Tech-Ideas Hub offers digital venture incubation, startup onboarding, and innovation support in partnership with the Startup Science platform, connecting early-stage ventures to mentorship, funding networks, and visibility.

  • Startup Science Tech Ideas Portal partnership
  • Digital skills training and international certification
  • Ideas-to-venture facilitation for youth innovators

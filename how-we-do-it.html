HOWWE DOIT.

From training design to jobs and enterprise outcomes

Approach

Venture creation & competent human capital

Our entrepreneurship and vocational skills training is built on our drive towards ensuring venture creation and the availability of competent human capital for jobs or entrepreneurship pathways.

We understand the importance of human capital development so we equip identified and willing individuals with the requisite mindset, skills and the requisite certification to enable them harness opportunities.

Training workshop photo placeholder
A.

Entrepreneurship & Skills Development Program (ESDP)

This is a training program that is an integral part of the school curriculum and a requirement for graduation. It consists of theoretical lessons on Entrepreneurship and soft skills, to equip undergraduate youths with relevant skills for job readiness or self reliance (via entrepreneurial practice) upon graduation.

E&I Centre Limited runs a yearly Vocational Skills Training and Entrepreneurship Promotion Program (Students V-STEP) for tertiary institution students.

B.

LED & Business Linkages

Working with clients and strategic partners, we build the capacity of enterprises within urban and rural areas equipping them with entrepreneurship competence and access to finance. This serves as a socioeconomic development catalyst project to reduce poverty by increasing income through employment generation and economic empowerment.

C.

Professional Construction Skills Training

As a Training, Business Linkages and Job Creation Organization, we partner with strategic stakeholders in the construction industry to run a competency based and demand driven skills development program.

  • Carpentry / Form Work
  • Plumbing
  • Tiling / Masonry
  • Welding & Fabrication
  • Electrical Wiring / Installation
  • Scaffolding
  • Entrepreneurship and soft skills training across board

Facilitation

Business Linkage Facilitation & Mentoring Workshops

We offer Business Linkage Facilitation business mentoring workshop to link individuals or SMEs to job opportunities. To achieve this, the program focuses on:

SME Capacity

Building the capacity of SMEs to address the limitations they have to competitively meet specific market opportunities.

Job & Market Linkages

Increasing linkages between skilled individuals to get employment/job opportunities and SMEs to acquire new markets.

Access to Finance

Improving access to finance for enterprises and aspiring entrepreneurs.

Consulting

Sustainable Development & Management Consultancy

E&I Centre Ltd. understands the Sustainable Development Goals (SDG) and can facilitate communities, organizations and individuals to achieve set objectives and targets.

We also specialize in Management Consultancy to help organizations improve their performance and growth.

Safety & Quality

HSEQ Services

E&I Centre Ltd., in collaboration with its partner company Herock Integrated Safety Services Ltd, delivers innovative, cost-effective health, safety, environment and quality consulting services to various clients particularly operators in the Nigerian Oil & Gas downstream sector.

From corporate-level HSEQ policy setting and risk management, to facility-level HSEQ outsourcing initiatives and support, EIC's client-focused, cost-effective and reliable HSE solutions improve operational efficiency, reduce risk, adding true business value.

Emerging Digital Literacy Skills Career Certification

Emerging Digital Literacy Skill career certification pathway provides an individual with everything they need, in this disruptive digital era, for a successful career of choice from start to finish, with Learning, Practice Test and Certification.

As a Certiport authorised testing centre, our digital skills training begins with a baseline assessment to test learner's current level of digital literacy/competence.

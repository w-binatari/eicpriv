About EIC

Creating sustainable jobs. EIC uses innovation-driven entrepreneurship and strategic partnership to support economic development and wealth creation.

Who we are

Entrepreneurship & Innovation Centre Ltd (EIC) is a professional Human Capital Development and management consultancy. We provide business and training services for economic empowerment and employment generation through local enterprise development and business linkages.

Based in Port Harcourt, we equip, identify, and align willing individuals with the mindset, skills, and certification needed to harness opportunities in jobs or entrepreneurship pathways.

Vision

To become a centre of excellence for developing highly competent human capital that will be economically self-reliant and employable through job and business linkages.

Mission

Creating sustainable jobs in Nigeria through Local Enterprise Development and Business Linkages.

About the Centre

We leverage innovation-driven entrepreneurship and strategic partnership to support sustainable economic development and wealth creation.

Entrepreneurship & Innovation Centre (E&I Centre Ltd) is a professional human capital development and management consultancy. We provide business and training services for economic empowerment and employment generation through local enterprise development and business linkages.

Based in Port Harcourt and affiliated with Rivers State University, the Centre serves as a hub for entrepreneurial education, innovation support, and capacity development — driving growth in Rivers State and across Nigeria.

Chika Chinwah is a management consultant, entrepreneur, and innovation leader with extensive experience driving organisational transformation and capacity development across Nigeria’s oil & gas, technology, and SME sectors.

As CEO of E&I Centre Ltd, he leads a team committed to unlocking entrepreneurial potential, strengthening innovation ecosystems, and delivering training for individuals and corporate organisations.

His work connects academia, industry, and government — positioning the Centre as a trusted hub for business development, startup support, and professional capacity building.

Who We Are

Building capacity for economic empowerment

Entrepreneurship & Innovation Centre Ltd. is a professional Human Capital Development (HCD) and management consultancy company that provides business and training services for economic empowerment and employment generation through local enterprise development and business linkages.

We leverage innovation driven entrepreneurship and strategic partnership to achieve sustainable economic development and wealth creation.

Organisation / training photo placeholder
Vision

To become a centre of excellence for developing highly competent human capital that will be economically self-reliant and employable through job and business linkages.

Mission

Creating sustainable jobs in Nigeria through Local Enterprise Development and Business Linkages.

Core Values

Our core values are deeply rooted in our desire to develop human capital and creating sustainable jobs.

About EIC

Entrepreneurship & Innovation Centre Ltd uses innovation-driven entrepreneurship and strategic partnership to support sustainable economic development and wealth creation.

Who we are

Entrepreneurship & Innovation Centre Ltd (EIC) is a professional human capital development and management consultancy. We provide business and training services for economic empowerment and employment generation through local enterprise development and business linkages.

Based in Port Harcourt, EIC serves as a hub for entrepreneurial education, innovation support, and capacity development across Rivers State and Nigeria.
